# Pizza Sales SQL Analysis

A SQL project analyzing transactional sales data from a pizza restaurant to uncover business insights around orders, revenue, and customer preferences.

## Tools Used
- **MySQL** — querying, joins, and aggregation
- **Dataset**: 4 relational tables — `orders`, `order_details`, `pizzas`, `pizza_types`

## Questions Answered
1. What is the total number of orders placed?
2. What is the total revenue generated from pizza sales?
3. Which pizza has the highest price?
4. What is the most common pizza size ordered?
5. What are the top 5 most ordered pizza types by quantity?
6. What is the total quantity ordered per pizza category?

Each question was solved using SQL techniques including `JOIN`, `GROUP BY`, `ORDER BY`, aggregate functions (`COUNT`, `SUM`, `ROUND`), and filtering with `LIMIT`.

## Key Insights
- 📦 **21,350** total orders were placed
- 💰 **$817,860.05** in total revenue generated
- 👑 **The Greek Pizza** is the highest-priced item at **$35.95**
- 📏 **Large (L)** is the most commonly ordered pizza size
- 🏆 **The Classic Deluxe Pizza** is the top-selling pizza by quantity (2,453 units)
- 🍕 **Classic** is the top-performing category by total quantity ordered (14,888 units)
